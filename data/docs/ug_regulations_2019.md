# 2 Academic Session
The academic year has Semester I (fourth week of July to last week of November), Semester II (last week of December to last week of April) and a Summer Term (mid-May to mid-July). A semester has at least 70 days of instruction excluding examinations. The Academic Calendar gives exact dates for registration, add/drop, examinations, results and recesses.

# 3 Registration
Students register for the prescribed credits on dates in the Academic Calendar; late registration is possible with a fine. A student who misses the late-registration date is deregistered; appeal goes to the Chairman, Senate through Dean Academics.
## 3.3 Adding and Dropping of Courses
A course can be added or dropped with the approval of the Faculty Advisor until the last date of registration.
## 3.4 Withdrawing from a Course
After add-drop closes a student may withdraw from a course, typically within four weeks of the start of the semester. A 'W' grade appears on the grade card.
## 3.5 Cancellation of Semester Registration
Absence of four or more weeks during a semester automatically cancels the student's registration in all courses of that semester.

# 4.1.5 Categories of Courses
A regular course has 14 hours of classroom engagement per credit. Elective courses of level 100 to 600 run only if at least 10 students register; 700/800-level electives need at least 5. A course that is not running may be taken as a self-study course (up to four credits per semester of independent/self study) with recommendation of the Course Coordinator and DUGC/DRC and sanction of the Dean (Academics). A student with CGPA of at least 6.0 may take an Independent Study course of at most 3 credits, once in the programme; it counts towards open electives.

# 4.1 Course Numbering
Course code example MEL1XX0: first two letters are the department (ME, CS, EE, CI, CH, BB, MT, CY, MA, PH, HS; platforms DH, SS, IO, AV, QC, CG, SH; offices OA, OS), third letter is the course type (L lecture, P practical, D project, N non-graded, T thesis, Q seminar, S independent study, R product realization, E engineering design), then the level digit. Level 100-400 are B.Tech. courses, 500 preparatory M.Sc./M.Tech., 600 M.Sc., 700 M.Tech., 800 advanced M.Tech./Ph.D. Fractal courses end in digits 1-3.

# 4.1.9 Attendance Requirements
A student is expected to attend every lecture, tutorial and lab unless on approved leave. Attendance records must be made available to the student after every lecture. Even if attendance falls below 75%, the student is still allowed to appear in the examinations. Students who do not meet the 75% attendance criterion must score at least a C grade to pass; they are awarded F if their marks are below the cut-off for C. (NOTE: an Amendment to the Attendance Policy applies to all students from Semester I of AY 2024-25 — the newest document must be preferred when they differ.)

# 4.2 Evaluation
Minor 1, Minor 2 and the Major examination are mandatory for a regular 14-week lecture course. Minors are 60 minutes and the Major is 120 minutes. The three examinations together carry 40% to 60% of the total weightage. Fractal courses (fewer than 3 credits) need at least one examination. The evaluation policy must be announced on the first day of class.

# 5.1 Letter Grades
Grades are A*, A, A-, B, B-, C, C-, D, E and F (A* and A carry 10 points, A- 9, B 8, B- 7, C 6, C- 5, D 4, E 2, F 0). Special grades: I, S, X, U, W.
A* is given only in courses with more than 50 students and to at most 2% of the class.
## Grade E
A student with an E grade (marks below the D cut-off but attendance satisfied) may appear in one additional examination held in the first week of the next semester; if successful E converts to D. At most three additional tests are allowed in a semester. E and F are not counted in CGPA but are counted in SGPA.
## Grade I
An I (incomplete) grade is given on request with the instructor's approval when an evaluation component is missed due to medical or special circumstances; the request must be made no later than the last day of major tests and requirements must be completed before the end of the first week of the next semester.

# 8 Make-up Examinations
A student who missed minor(s) or the major examination due to genuine reasons like illness may be permitted a make-up examination. The student must apply to the Dean (Academics) through the course instructor within ten days from the date of the examination missed, explaining the reasons. Applications after this period need not be entertained. Permission is given under exceptional circumstances such as hospital admission. Documents: hostel residents need a medical certificate from the Institute Medical Officer; those consulting outside doctors need written permission/endorsement from the Institute Medical Officer; students living off campus need a certificate from a Registered Medical Practitioner endorsed by parent/guardian. The slot-wise make-up examination for major is held in the make-up/supplementary week of the next semester per the Academic Calendar. A missed make-up exam is normally not repeated; in prolonged illness the Chairman Senate with Dean Academics may allow it before the end of first week of next semester. Prorating of missed exams or attendance is not allowed.

# 11.1 Academic Load and Credits (UG)
Credits = (lecture hours + tutorial hours + 0.5 x lab hours + project hours); one lecture hour is 1 credit, one lab hour 0.5 credit.

# 11.3 Course Categories and Credit Requirements (B.Tech.)
Regular B.Tech. needs 150 graded credits plus 15 non-graded credits, i.e. 165 credits in total. Institute Core: Engineering 34, Science 16, Humanities 12. Programme Linked Science 7. Programme Compulsory 50, Programme Electives 18, B.Tech. Project 3. Open Electives (OE) 10 credits for regular B.Tech. (0 for B.Tech. with specialization, which instead needs 8 compulsory and 12 elective specialization credits: 160 graded and 175 total). Dual degree needs 196 graded, 211 total. Non-graded: Humanities 6, Engineering 3, Design/Practical Experience 6.

# 11.2 Capability linked opportunities
Minor/Specialization: at least 100 credits with CGPA 6.0 or more at the end of the 6th semester; 20 credits in the specialization area (10 may count as open electives). Dual degree needs 134 credits and CGPA 6.0 at the end of the 7th semester.

# 11.4 Maximum Duration
The maximum duration for B.Tech. is 14 registered semesters.

# 11.5 Continuation Criteria
At the end of the second registered semester a student must have earned at least 18 credits (GEN/OBC/GEN-EWS) or 14 credits (SC/ST/PD) to continue; otherwise the student may restart (once) or be terminated.

# 12.2 Limits on Registration (UG)
An undergraduate student registers for at least 12 and at most 25 credits per semester. Summer registration is limited to 8 credits. Under-load (academic probation) maximum is 16 credits. Overload needs CGPA 7.5 or higher beyond the second semester, or 70 or fewer credits remaining.

# 14 Termination from B.Tech.
Termination can result from eight or more weeks of absence without authorized leave, failing to register without a bonafide reason, code of conduct violations or ragging, not earning minimum credits within the maximum duration, or two consecutive semesters on academic probation. Appeal lies with the Chairman, Senate.

# 15 Leave Rules
Applications for leave of absence are addressed to the Convener DUGC with a medical certificate where applicable; leave should not be taken without prior approval.
## 15.2 Short Leave
A student may be granted leave of absence during a semester for a maximum of 10 days on medical grounds and 5 days for any other valid reason.
## 15.3 Semester Withdrawal
Absence of four or more weeks in a semester means automatic withdrawal from that semester; medical semester withdrawal does not count towards the maximum duration.
## 15.4 Academic Leave
For fieldwork, specialized experiments, entrepreneurship, product realization, exchange programmes, internships; approved by DUGC; at most one summer term and two semesters; up to 20 credits per semester can be earned.

# 16 Branch Change
Allowed only after the first year (after semester II) with all credits including non-graded earned, no backlog and no disciplinary action. CGPA of at least 8.5 (7.5 for SC/ST/PwD) for any branch; for Engineering Science CGPA at least 8 (7 for SC/ST/PwD). No branch change from the second year onwards.

# 17 Graduation Requirements
Minimum residence is 6 semesters for B.Tech. and 8 for B.Tech.-M.Tech. The student must earn the required credits, clear No Dues, have no pending disciplinary case, and complete at least two semesters of NCC, NSS, NSO, sports or performing arts engagement.
