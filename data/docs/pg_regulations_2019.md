# 21.1 Limits on PG Registration
M.Sc. students register for at most 24 and at least 10 credits per semester. Full-time regular/sponsored M.Tech. students register for 10 to 17 credits in a regular semester. Ph.D. full-time students register for 6 to 12 credits.

# 22.3 M.Sc. Credit Requirements
M.Sc. in Chemistry, Mathematics or Physics requires 77 credits: 38 compulsory, 18 electives, 6 open electives, 15 project.

# 22.4 M.Tech. Credit Requirements
Total minimum credits: M.Tech. Artificial Intelligence 62 (18 MC, 18 ME, 6 MO, 16 project, 4 non-graded); M.Tech. Computer Science and Engineering 62; Sensors and IoT 62; Cyber Physical Systems 62; Thermofluids Engineering 60; Advanced Manufacturing Engineering and Design 60; Metallurgical and Materials Engineering 60; Bioscience and Bioengineering 61; Data and Computational Sciences 61. Every M.Tech. programme includes 6 open-elective credits.

# 22.7 Ph.D. Course Work
A Ph.D. student with an M.Tech./M.E./M.Sc.(Engg) needs at least 12 course credits and 48 thesis credits. With a B.Tech./B.E. or a science M.Sc. in an engineering discipline the course work is 30 credits and thesis 48.

# 22.8 Minimum CGPA
M.Tech. needs CGPA 5 for continuation and graduation; assistantship continuation needs 6.5. M.Tech.-Ph.D. dual degree needs 7. Ph.D. needs 7 (7.5 at completion of coursework).

# 23 Leave Rules (PG)
Applications for leave go to the Convener DRC. A PG student may take at most 30 days of leave in an academic year including medical leave and mid-semester break; M.Tech./M.Sc. students cannot take more than 10 days at a stretch and Ph.D. students not more than 15 days at a stretch. Absence of four or more weeks in a semester means automatic semester withdrawal and the fellowship stops.

# 21.5 Maximum Duration
M.Sc. 8 registered semesters; M.Tech. 12 semesters (including at most 6 of Registration Kept Alive); Ph.D. and M.Tech.-Ph.D. 14 semesters.
