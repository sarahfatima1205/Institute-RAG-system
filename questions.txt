IIT Jodhpur Academic Helpdesk - Test Questions
================================================

A. Academic policy (rules and regulations)
------------------------------------------
Q01. What is the attendance requirement to sit in the Major exam?
     Expected: 75% attendance in a course, as per the 2024 attendance amendment (not the withdrawn older rule).

Q02. How many Open Elective credits do I need to earn in order to get a B.Tech. degree?
     Expected: 10 credits.

Q03. How many total credits are needed to graduate in regular B.Tech.?
     Expected: 165 credits (150 graded + 15 non-graded).

Q04. Within how many days must I apply for a make-up exam after missing it?
     Expected: Within ten days of the missed examination.

Q05. What is the maximum short leave allowed on medical grounds for a B.Tech. student?
     Expected: Up to 10 days per semester.

Q06. How many credits must a GEN student earn by the end of the second semester to continue the B.Tech.?
     Expected: 18 credits.

Q07. What CGPA is needed for branch change after the first year?
     Expected: CGPA 8.5 (7.5 for SC/ST/PwD).

Q08. What are the minimum and maximum credits a UG student can register in a semester?
     Expected: Minimum 12, maximum 25 credits.

Q09. What happens to a student who gets an E grade?
     Expected: The student can appear in an additional examination.

Q10. How many total credits does the M.Tech. in Artificial Intelligence need?
     Expected: 62 credits.

Q11. How many course credits does a PhD student with an M.Tech. need?
     Expected: 12 credits.

Q12. What happens if I am absent for four weeks in a semester?
     Expected: Registration is cancelled / the student is withdrawn from the semester.

Q13. What is the maximum duration allowed to complete a B.Tech.?
     Expected: 14 semesters.
